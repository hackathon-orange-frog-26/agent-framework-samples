Subject: Workshop - Invoice Automation Requirements

Design supplier invoice automation for Contoso Demo:
- Process 10,000 invoices each business day, with a peak of 50 per minute.
- Receive PDF invoices, extract fields, validate against ERP purchase orders,
  route exceptions to a human, and write approved records back to the ERP.
- Require two separate approvers for invoices above EUR 10,000.
- Prevent duplicate payments and retain an auditable approval history.
- Classify invoice data as internal synthetic data in this workshop.
- Treat the production design as tier 1: RTO one hour and RPO 15 minutes.
- Keep data services private; use managed identities where supported.
- Target EUR 5,000 per month initially; state pricing assumptions and tradeoffs.
- Do not deploy resources or send approval notifications during the demo.
